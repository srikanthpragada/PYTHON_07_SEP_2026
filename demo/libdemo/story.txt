Once upon a time, in a quiet village, there lived a little girl named Mia who loved watching the moon every night.
One evening, she noticed that the moon looked sad. Why are you sad, Moon? she whispered.
To her surprise, a gentle voice answered, I shine brightly, but I am lonely up here in the sky. 
Mia smiled and said,  Don't worry! I will talk to you every night. 
From that day on, Mia waved to the moon before going to bed. She told it stories, sang songs, and shared her little dreams.
The moon became brighter and happier each night. It began shining its soft silver light over the village, helping children feel safe as they slept.
One night, Mia looked up and saw a tiny, sparkling star beside the moon.
 Look! she exclaimed.  You have a friend now!
The moon smiled and whispered, And you will always be my friend, too.
From then on, whenever Mia saw the moon glowing in the night sky, she knew that even the loneliest heart could find happiness through kindness and friendship.
Moral: A little kindness can brighten even the darkest night.
